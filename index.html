§ 01The premise

Our path to RSI starts with infrastructure.

AI research depends on the infrastructure beneath it. Improving that infrastructure gives researchers more room to experiment.

The work spans architecture, execution and kernels; every step is checked for correctness, speed and training quality.

§ 05The long-term direction

AI that improves the tools that improve AI.

ResearchBoost also offers a concrete starting point for studying recursive self-improvement (RSI): long-horizon, technically demanding tasks with relatively clear feedback, spanning system-level architecture and low-level GPU kernels.

For us, this is a practical starting point: learning how AI can improve the tools and workflows that advance AI research.
