### Change 1.1 — FlashAttention over the split layout (BAGEL_OPT_FAST_ATTN=1), tier N1

modeling/bagel/fast_attn.py (new): the mask of a sample is determined by its splits (a query split sees the
prefix of its sample that ends with the split: causal for text, bidirectional for an image span; a noise split is
invisible to later splits). Each query split is one varlen sequence of FlashAttention: two calls per step
(causal / bidirectional) over all samples, key/value rows gathered once per layer. Query splits after a noise
split (2% of the samples) keep upstream's SDPA on their rows of the mask. The split layout is attached to the mask
tensor when the dataset builds it (data/dataset_base.py), carried through pin_memory/cuda; the batches
(fields and bytes) are unchanged. Deterministic mode passes deterministic=True.

Checked on one GPU (/tmp/analysis/test_fast_attn.py): 10 layouts × deterministic on/off, forward and gradients
against upstream's SDPA and an exact fp32 reference: same max error as SDPA's (ratio ≤ 1.04), no NaN.
Speed on the profiled layout (59M tokens², forward + backward incl. the K/V expansion): SDPA 26.7 ms → 8.4 ms.
