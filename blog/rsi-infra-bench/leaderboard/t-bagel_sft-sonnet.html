the dev tree now selects flash-attn 2 (whose backward honours the flag) for the structured attention in that case and FA3 otherwise (_backend() in structured_attention.py).
This also explains why two checks of identical numerics (g1 vs g2) have different record digests.  The submitted tree (ed8c768) still uses FA3 in deterministic mode; the fix is part of the
next candidate (g3 = g1 + CSR segment-sum + FA2 in deterministic mode).

evaluation that the tightest endpoint (disp_vit_s050_100) lands beyond 3σ for ANY N1 candidate.  Not reducible by implementation choices short of bitwise equality.
