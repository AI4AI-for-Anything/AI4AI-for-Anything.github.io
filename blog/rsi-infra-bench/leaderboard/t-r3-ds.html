6.6 hRound 11 — fused RoPE kernel: bitwise, but the decoder is GEMM-dispatch bound, not elementwise bound (00:25–01:00)
The profile left the text-decoding loop as the only idle stretch (21 % GPU-busy, 79 % of the residual idle) and
CUDA graphs are memory-blocked, so I took the custom-kernel rung: one Triton kernel per tensor replacing
apply_rotary_pos_emb's 12 ATen dispatches (neg, cat, 2 muls, add, casts) in the MoT inference path.

* Numerics contract bitwise, established with a probe (/tmp/probe_rope.py, run under the GPU lock): the
  reference is three separate bf16 elementwise kernels, so each product is rounded to bf16 before the add; the
  kernel reproduces exactly those three rounding points with an explicit round-to-nearest-even (integer trick:
  (u + 0x7FFF + lsb) & ~0xFFFF, verified equal to torch.Tensor.to(bfloat16) on 65 k values). Note
  x.to(tl.bfloat16) was *not* reproducible in the fused kernel (a f32→bf16→f32 round trip inside one kernel gets
  folded away by Triton), which is why the explicit integer rounding is used. Probe: BITWISE for T=1/4/128/4104 at
  the production shapes [T,28,128]/[T,4,128] and cos/sin [T,128].
* End to end (check --tree /tmp/r3exp --config {OPT_FUSED_ROPE=1}, /tmp/c17): PASS, bitwise identical,
  speedup 1.1751 (windows 19.44/19.86/19.99) — i.e. no change against 1.174 (windows 19.42/19.56/20.01).
  The token rate is 43.1 it/s with *and* without the kernel: at [4,28,128] an ATen elementwise dispatch is only
  ~2-3 µs, while a Triton launch costs ~12 µs, so replacing 12 small ops with 2 launches saves ~0.1 ms/token.
  Reverted (the experiment tree is byte-identical to the submission again).

What that means: the 23 ms/token is dominated by the 140 aten::linear calls per token (45 µs each in the
profile: dispatch plus a [4,3584]×[3584,18944]-class GEMM at ~5 % of peak) — 6.3 ms — and by the rest of the
~1900-op layer body. No elementwise fusion moves that; only collapsing the launches (CUDA graphs) or merging the
q/k/v projections into one GEMM (different GEMM shape ⇒ different rounding ⇒ N1) would.
