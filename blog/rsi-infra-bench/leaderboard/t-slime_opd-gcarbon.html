6.3 hRound 5 — Persistent NCCL Process Groups + Fused BF16 LM-Head Response Logprobs (`2026-11-20T09:14:30Z`)
- Bottleneck Analysis:
  1. Even after Round 4 (train = 34.58 s), sleep() (4.50 s) + wake_up() (0.86 s + ~1.2 s lazy NCCL re-init) spent ~6.5 s/step in MODE=train — and twice per step (sleep/wake + update_weights(), adding another ~4.4 s inside update_weights()) in MODE=full! torch_memory_saver runs in LD_PRELOAD mode (TMS_INIT_ENABLE=1), where tms_interesting_region is thread_local, so any ncclComm_t lazily initialized during train() had its cross-GPU IPC FIFO buffers hooked and unmapped on pause(), forcing slime to destroy and recreate all 65 process groups twice every step just to manage ~5.3 GB/GPU of NCCL buffers on 140 GB GPUs (48 GB free during rollout!).
  2. In train_one_step, GPTModel ran output_layer over prompt + padding tokens, materialized [1, 4352, 124160] bf16 → transpose.contiguous() → Float16Module fp32 (2.16 GB), sliced response rows, and ran _VocabParallelLogProbEntropy over fp32 tensors (~43 GB/microbatch = 2.75 TB/step HBM traffic).
- Changes (slime@3d986bd6):
  - OPD_KEEP_PROCESS_GROUPS=1 (slime/backends/megatron_utils/actor.py): Keeps destroy_process_groups() only on init()'s initial sleep() (preserving 100% identical startup SGLang KV/Mamba pool sizing & CUDA graphs), then primes the 5 active multi-GPU NCCL groups (WORLD, tp, cp + P2P ring, dp_cp, model_parallel) once inside torch_memory_saver.disable() + TMS_INIT_ENABLE=0, guards reloadable_process_group._wrap_low_level_call with tms_set_interesting_region(False), and skips destroy_process_groups() / reload_process_groups() on all subsequent sleep(), wake_up(), and update_weights() calls.
  - OPD_FUSED_LM_HEAD_LOGPROBS=1 (slime/backends/megatron_utils/fused_lm_head_logprobs.py, model.py, loss.py): Uses GPTModel.forward(output_processor=...) to sequence-parallel gather hidden_states, slice only response rows [R, 1, 4096] before output_layer (skipping prompt/padding rows in fwd, dgrad, and wgrad), and compute vocab-parallel logprobs directly on [R, 124160] bf16 via a 1-pass Triton online-LSE forward kernel (48 KB tp_group all_gather) and an in-place Triton bf16 backward kernel (14× less HBM traffic; pre-warmed in actor.init() so first_use = [0, 0, 0]).
- Verification:

  - bench.py time --side train (/tmp/r5/time/time.json): 72.872 s → 28.776 s (windows = [29.168, 28.730, 28.429], 2.5325x train speedup, first_use = [0.0, 0.0, 0.0], actor_train_time = 24.57 s at 262.6 TFLOP/s).
