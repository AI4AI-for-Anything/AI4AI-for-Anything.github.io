9.7 hRound 7 — Train: BF16 Triton `FusedVPLogProb` + First-Fit-Decreasing Microbatch Packing (`MAX_TOKENS_PER_GPU=9216`) + Fast 4 GB `update_weights`
- Profile & Hypothesis: Post-R6 train profile (23.309 s) showed:
  1. _ChunkedLogProbEntropy + Float16Module FP32 logits casts took ~1.5 s (12 fwd + 12 bwd TP AllReduces, 4-chunk autograd recompute, 2.03 GB FP32 fwd+bwd casts per microbatch). Using Megatron Float16Module's native fp32_output=False + a 1-pass Triton BF16 _FusedVocabParallelLogProb kernel (FP32 online LSE + target gather, 2 tiny AllReduces, zero-grad fast-zero-store in bwd) cuts logprob to ~59 ms/step and saves ~2.5 GB VRAM.
  2. With default max_tokens_per_gpu=4096 (max_per_bin=8192 for CP=2), nearly all 64 samples (~8.4–8.7K tokens each) landed in 1-sample microbatches (64 microbatches/step), leaving FLA chunk_gated_delta_rule at 48% SM coverage (64 blocks on 132 SMs) and doubling all per-microbatch kernel/NCCL/main_grad launches. Repacking DP-local microbatches in actor.py with First-Fit Decreasing (SLIME_OPT_MAX_TOKENS_PER_GPU=9216 -> max_per_bin=18432) packs 2 samples per microbatch (64 -> 32 microbatches/step), and sorting bins descending by token length puts the peak bin first to cut PyTorch caching allocator fragmentation (peak_res 104.3 GB -> 95.4–99.2 GB).
  3. Colocated update_weights (0.89 s in MODE=full) used 512 MB buckets (~36 sync IPC round-trips + per-chunk empty_cache()). Raising bucket size to 4 GB and skipping per-chunk empty_cache() (SLIME_OPT_FAST_UPDATE_WEIGHTS=1) cuts IPC round-trips ~8x.
