of the cuBLASLt heuristic on every call (and queries the heuristic every call).  A ctypes cuBLASLt harness (/tmp/mb/lt.py, lt_bench.py, lt_scan.py, lt_retune.py) showed:
  * algo 0 of the heuristic list is bit-identical to torch._scaled_mm (all 12 shapes of the model); every candidate algorithm without split-K produced bit-identical outputs too
    (same K order / promotion interval), and all are deterministic -> the tuned table costs NO numerics (GEMM outputs are bitwise those of torch._scaled_mm);

    backward GEMMs (dgrad/wgrad, e5m2 x e4m3) are already at the best algorithm (heuristic default wins or is within 1 % when ranked under a mixed GEMM + memory-bound load);
  * sustained-load rankings are misleading (the real step interleaves memory-bound kernels, so clocks are higher): the final ranking (lt_retune.py) times each GEMM with a memory-bound filler in
