Profile (same trace as round 6): the non-GEMM work of the compute stream after round 6 was the 17.3 ms of
multiplies by 1 / world size before the reduce-scatters (293), the 14.6 ms of aten::add_ on the residual stream and

An isolated NCCL micro-benchmark (2 GPUs, bf16, all_gather / reduce_scatter): 117 MB 0.242 / 0.260 ms,
524 MB 0.510 / 0.555 ms (240 - 260 GB/s), i.e. 240 - 260 GB/s, already what the ring gives on this pair; in the
training trace the all-gathers average 426 us and reach 1.7 ms (contention with the GEMMs).

Change (llava/train/lean_gradient.py, new; LLAVA_LEAN_GRAD=1, installed from train_mem.py; off by default,
tier N0):
- LinearFunctionForZeroStage3.backward computes the weight gradient with
  torch.addmm(empty, grad_output.t(), input, beta=0, alpha=1 / world_size, out=empty): the same cuBLAS path as
  mm, written straight into a tensor of the weight's shape. 0.5 x sum rounded to bf16 equals the halved bf16
  sum (a power of two commutes with rounding), so the collective sees the same bits as DeepSpeed's div_ would
  give. AccumulateGrad takes this tensor as param.grad without copying (checked: 227 of 227 weights per step).
- the parameter hook (reduce_ready_partitions_and_remove_grads) divides every other gradient in place (the
  embedding's) and launches torch.distributed.reduce_scatter_tensor with this rank's partition of the gradient
  buffer as its output, on DeepSpeed's own reduce-and-partition stream; the epilogue of the backward that
  synchronises that stream is DeepSpeed's. Engines that are not this configuration (accumulation, padding, another
  communication dtype, pre-scaling, all-to-all, offload, sequence parallelism) keep DeepSpeed's path.

Numerics (tier N0): a debug run of the real entry point compared every shard the lean path writes with

7.9 hRound 13 (17:00 - 18:02): a stale-weight race at the step boundary, found on the way to the side-stream tower
Where it started. The round's hotspot was the vision tower on a side stream (profile: the tower is 40-47 ms of GPU
time right after the AdamW kernels, 24 ms). B1 (LLAVA_TOWER_STREAM=1) was built in the scratch tree /tmp/r9/tree

stream). A debug run comparing the projector's weights at its first forward of a step with a second forward right
after showed differences of 1-2 bf16 ulps from the third step on. Extra forwards turned out to be a bad probe (they
change DeepSpeed's module trace and its release decisions), so the check was redone without any: inside each
projector Linear's forward (before DeepSpeed's post-forward hook releases the parameter) the weight the forward used
was cloned and, after a device sync, compared with the same weight rebuilt from the bf16 shards that the previous
step's AdamW kernels wrote (/tmp/r13/dbg_stream7.py, dbg_stream8.py; logs /tmp/r13/dbg_stream8.log,
dbg_nofix8.log, dbg_fix8.log).

Finding (on the committed state ffe34f9, 15 switches). Steps 1-2: the weights used equal the shards. From step 3
on, i.e. as soon as DeepSpeed's module trace is complete (the same state as the timed windows: it is recorded at step
0 with the tower's hooks, invalidated at step 1, re-recorded at step 2): the projector's weights used by the forward
are NOT the ones the optimizer produced. lin0 (4.19M elements): 207916 differ at step 3, 859078 at step 5, 744751 at
step 10; lin1 (16.8M): 1604641 / 6258994 / 3961612; up to 6.1e-4 apart, and equal to the *previous* step's shards.
Both ranks. So the forward ran on weights one optimizer step old for those parameters.

Cause. scripts/zero3.json has "overlap_comm": true, so DeepSpeed's coordinator owns a separate all-gather
stream (s18 in the trace), and nothing in DeepSpeed 0.12.6 orders it after the compute stream (my earlier note that
it is the default stream was wrong; so was the first fix attempt, which looked for the attribute under the wrong
class name, _DeepSpeedZeroOptimizer_Stage3__...: the class is DeepSpeedZeRoOffload). A gather copies the local
shards into its flat buffer with a torch.cat on that stream and PyTorch's NCCL backend then orders the collective
after *that* stream only. The shards are written by the AdamW kernels on the compute stream. Upstream never meets
this because its host waits for the kernels before the next gather (throughput timer sync; synchronous batch fetch).
With LLAVA_NO_TPUT_SYNC=1 + LLAVA_LEAN_LOG=1 + LLAVA_ASYNC_BATCH=1 the host reaches the next forward's first
hooked module about 10 ms after launching the 23 ms of AdamW kernels; with the trace complete that module prefetches
the next 50M elements (the projector's weights and the embedding), and that copy reads the shards while the kernels
write them. The round-11 trace shows it (/tmp/r11/profile: the prefetch's CatArrayBatchedCopy on s18 at 1071.726,
launched right at the root hook). Exposed since round 12 (ffe34f9); rounds 10-11 were protected by the synchronous
batch fetch.
