cos 0.991 - 0.994, i.e. its run-to-run noise is at fp32 level. A change that alters bf16 activation values, even
one unit in the last place on 6e-6 of one norm's outputs per layer, spreads within a few layers to whole token

dead work, thread counts, memory and offload strategy, scheduling, and fp32-level changes of gradient
reductions and the optimizer. The rollout side is judged statistically (round-5 check: ratios 1.00 - 1.01).
