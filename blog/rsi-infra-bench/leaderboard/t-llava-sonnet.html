5.1 hRound 15 — label-balanced partition, LLAVAOPT_BALANCE=2 (13:25-13:45)
- Earlier BALANCE=1 (token-balanced) rejected because it moves the mean-of-micro-batch-means loss (gate-3 canonical endpoint). New idea:
  choose, per step, the split of the 32 samples into two 16-sample micro-batches with equal SUPERVISED-token counts (|dN| <= 12) and then
  equal compute cost (tokens + tokens^2/12000): then the mean of the two token means IS the step's global token mean, whatever the partition.
  Measured at the reference partitions (/tmp/ana/mom_gap.py, 24 steps, initial weights): MoM - global mean: mean 0.001, std 0.005 per step
  (max 0.011) -> over 20 steps ~0.001 +- 0.001 << bound 0.0094. Observed in the full check: d = +0.584 sigma = +0.0018.
- _token_modality_lengths (train.py) tokenizes every sample once (~30 s at startup, not timed) to get token length + label count;
  _label_balanced_chunks (llava_trainer.py) = greedy + best-improvement swaps. Data-probe multisets identical for all 8 steps.
