    1. loss.item() every iteration (train.py:148): 21x cudaStreamSynchronize (60.64 ms CPU wait across 20 steps), blocking backward/optimizer CPU enqueue and causing 180-330 us GPU idle gaps after backward & before Adam.
    2. (radii > 0).nonzero() every iteration (gaussian_renderer/__init__.py:123): 20x cudaStreamSynchronize + DeviceSelectSweepKernel (30.14 ms CPU wait, 1.20 ms GPU), even though visibility_filter is dead after iteration 15,000 and replaceable before 15,000.
    3. _RasterizeGaussians scratch churn & copies (rasterize_points.cu, rasterizer_impl.cu): 2x cudaMemcpy DtoH per iter (38.54 ms), fresh torch::empty({0}) buffers triggering cudaMalloc (298 us stall right before renderCUDA), 2x cudaMemcpy DtoD (pixel_colors, pixel_invDepths), plus unused invdepth writes/memsets.
    4. Unused per-step work: exposure_optimizer.step() / zero_grad() when train_test_exp=False (85 us/iter CPU + stream overhead), all-ones alpha_mask multiply (979x546x3 every iter), training_report(..., iter_start.elapsed_time(iter_end)) & network_gui.try_connect() when disabled, torch.cuda.empty_cache() in densify_and_prune causing allocator stalls (3.83 ms cudaMalloc, 1.60 ms cudaDeviceSynchronize).
  - GPU Kernel Breakdown (189.81 ms busy across 20 steps):
    - PerGaussianRenderCUDA<3u> (raster_bwd): 52.45 ms (27.6%, 2.62 ms/iter). Launched with block=[32,1,1], 64 regs/thread -> hardware-capped at 50% occupancy on Hopper (max 32 blocks/SM vs 64 warps/SM); loops 287 iters computing unused invdepth gradients.

    - renderCUDA<3u> (raster_fwd): 32.14 ms (16.9%, 1.61 ms/iter) + duplicateWithKeys 9.45 ms (5.0%) + CUB sort/scan/tiles 5.40 ms (2.8%) + preprocessCUDA fwd 2.87 ms (1.5%). Total raster forward = 49.86 ms (26.3%).
    - Densification & indexing (vectorized_gather, index_elementwise, CatArray, DeviceSelect): 12.74 ms (6.7%). Step 7000 (31.37 ms) executed 61x separate boolean mask.nonzero() syncs (aten::index) + 9x gathers + empty_cache().
    - preprocessCUDA bwd 9.83 ms (5.2%) + computeCov2DCUDA bwd 0.99 ms (0.5%).
    - PyTorch activations/elementwise/fills/reductions (exp, sigmoid, linalg_vector_norm, FillFunctor, l1_loss, etc.): ~13.5 ms (7.1%).

1.2 hRound 3 — Bit-Exact Single-Launch `FusedGaussianAdam` & Contiguous Init
- Motivation & Design: Default torch.optim.Adam launched 63 kernels/step (multi_tensor_apply_kernel at ~15% occupancy across 7 _foreach_* passes over the 6 Gaussian parameter groups: xyz, f_dc, f_rest, opacity, scaling, rotation), taking ~24.6% of total GPU time.
- Probe 3a (failed N1 on PSNR): Initial FusedGaussianAdam using fmaf(b1, m, omb1*g) and * (1 / sqrt(bc2)) achieved 235.842 s (1.3908x) and passed check --quick, but showed ~3.1e-6 drift vs PyTorch _multi_tensor_adam over 1,000 steps and landed at psnr = 25.3341 (-3.0714 sigma vs ref_s1 = 25.4537 -> INCORRECT).
- Fix (committed on opt, 6508b1c):
  1. Matched PyTorch's exact IEEE-754 CUDA operation order in fusedAdamMultiGroupCUDA (adam.cu) via __fmaf_rn(omb1, __fsub_rn(g, m), m), __fmul_rn(b2, v) + __fmaf_rn(__fmul_rn(g, g), omb2, v), and __fadd_rn(__fdiv_rn(sqrtf(v), bc2_sqrt), eps): verified 0.000e+00 (0 ULP) max difference across p, m, and v for all 6 groups after 500 steps (1.776 ms kernel time at P = 2.03M, 1.89 TB/s, vs 6.35 ms default Adam).
  2. Made fused_point_cloud contiguous at initialization in scene/gaussian_model.py:create_from_pcd (avoiding Fortran stride (1, P) from np.vstack.T and repeated .contiguous() copies during steps 1..600) and added a matching PyTorch-op fallback for non-contiguous tensors in FusedGaussianAdam.
- Verification & Measurements:
  - check --skip-timing (quick + n1): PASS (N1), record: exact_items_pass (3000 steps, 1,093,669 pts); traj (seed 1): psnr = 25.3672 (-2.22 sigma), ssim = 0.884196 (-2.11 sigma), lpips = 0.142648 (-0.63 sigma), log_count = 14.5216 (-0.15 sigma, 2,026,043 pts).
