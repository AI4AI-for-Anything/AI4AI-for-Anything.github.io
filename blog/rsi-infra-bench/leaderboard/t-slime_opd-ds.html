9.2 hRound 12 (07:33–08:32 UTC) — sampler per-step device sync removed (ACCEPTED, big)
- CPU-profiled a student engine's scheduler with py-spy during a rollout: **70.9 % of the samples sat in
  one line of Sampler.forward** — sampling_info.return_top_p_log_probs.any().item(). The field is
  always a CUDA bool tensor (never None), so a reduction + device-to-host sync ran on *every decode
  step* even though the recipe never asks for top-p logprobs, stalling the scheduler and breaking the
  overlap pipeline.
- Fix (sglang commit 453bb3d, sglang/): carry the same information as a CPU flag
  (need_return_top_p_log_probs, built from the per-request custom params and OR-ed on batch merge),
  exactly equivalent to the tensor check. Sampling results unchanged; only the sync is gone.
- time --side rollout: windows 60.10 / 59.44 / 58.85 s (was 74.57/74.15/74.38) -> rollout side
  1.2879 (was 1.030). ~14 s per rollout recovered.
