2.9 hRound 5 — why head-parallel GDN fails the gate, and a numerics-preserving replacement (12:10–)
- In-run comparison on real weights/data (OPT_GDN_HP_DEBUG, /tmp/r3/hpdbg1): HP out / dx / param grads match the replicated layer to 0.3-1.5% (bf16 noise) -> the
  math is right; HP simply injects bf16-rounding noise into every layer's activations and activation gradients (partial sums of 4 head groups rounded to bf16,
  different GEMM shapes) and the update-direction cosine gate (cos >= 0.955 = 1 - 5 x the reference's own 1-cos of 0.009) amplifies it through the 32 layers
  (lower layers / embedding: cos 0.84).  => on the training side only (near-)bitwise-equivalent changes can pass this gate.
- Microbenchmark (/home/agent/work/dev/gemm_mindep.py): F.linear for the GDN projection shapes (qkv, z, out_proj; N=8192/4096) is BITWISE independent of the token
  count M (forward and dgrad, M/2, M/4, M/8); only the tiny N=32 b/a projections differ.
- New design OPT_GDN_ULYSSES (qwen3_5_hp.py::forward_ulysses): token-parallel norm + projections on the LOCAL tokens (1/4 of the GEMM work, no all-gather of x),
  all-to-all to head-parallel layout, the reference's own per-head code (conv, gating, fla gated delta rule, gated norm) on 8 of 32 heads over the full sequence,
  all-to-all back, out_proj on the local tokens (no reduce-scatter).  Activations and activation gradients are the reference's values bit for bit (same ops, M-independent
  GEMMs); only weight gradients are summed over 4 token chunks instead of 2 (+ b/a projections with different M).

7.5 hRound 10 — skip the backward of micro-batches with all-zero advantages (16:50–)
- Observation (per-micro-batch layer-output gradients, Round 5b): in the fixture's rollouts 56 of the 64 micro-batches (1 sample each) have an EXACTLY zero gradient: all rewards of
  their group are equal -> advantages 0 (GRPO without std normalization) -> the REINFORCE loss, the score-centering correction and the TIS weights are all proportional to the
  advantages; entropy and KL coefficients are 0.  Their backward (~2/3 of the micro-batch compute) multiplies zeros.  Real rollouts of this recipe (8K-token responses, rewards mostly 0)
  have the same structure.
- OPT_SKIP_ZERO_ADV (slime/backends/megatron_utils/model.py): when the micro-batch's advantages are all zero (decision all-reduced over the TP x CP group), the model forward runs under
