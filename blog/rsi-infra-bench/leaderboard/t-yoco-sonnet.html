6.8 hRound 12 — grad norm + convert on the critical path; alignment hints for Triton pointer-table kernels (22:30 - 23:00)
Profile /tmp/r17/profile: after the backward the critical chain is norm -> clip math -> convert -> first Adam group. Findings: apex
multi_tensor_l2norm = 165 launches of ~18 us + 3 us gaps = 3.16 ms (2.2 TB/s); the convert needs scale = float(mf.item()) (host sync) and
then ~480 us of host launch work: ~520 us of GPU idle between the end of the norm/clip kernels and the first convert kernel; convert
5.7 ms (3.6 TB/s).
- Why the earlier Triton pointer-table kernels (TOPT) never beat apex: tensors reached through a descriptor table have unknown alignment, so

9.5 hRound 17 — fp32 master gradients on demand (01:25 - 01:40)
Profile /tmp/r26/profile_best: the optimizer phase is 31 of 107.5 ms; after the backward the chain is norm (1.5 ms) -> clip -> **convert of every bf16

  a plain fp32 tensor); the convert kernel of the update is queued as a closure and runs on the first access of any LazyGrad of that update (any
  torch operation or attribute goes through __torch_function__, which materializes all of them with the same Triton convert and then runs the
  operation on plain tensors); zero_grad discards a pending update. The code path is chosen by the configuration (TOPT + READ16 + PIPE), never by who
  is calling; a training step that does not read the fp32 gradients does not pay for them, everybody who reads them gets the reference
